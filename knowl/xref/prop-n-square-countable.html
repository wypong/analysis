Proposition A.1.8.

The set \(\N^2\) is countable.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Define \(P:\N^2\to\N\) by \(P(j,m)=2^{j-1}(2m-1)\text{.}\) Every \(k\in\N\) has a unique factorization \(k=2^r q\text{,}\) where \(r\geq0\) and \(q\) is odd. Thus \(k=P(j,m)\) for the unique pair \(j=r+1\) and \(m=(q+1)/2\text{.}\) Therefore \(P\) is bijective.
