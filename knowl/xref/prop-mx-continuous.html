Proposition 4.1.4.

For any \(m \in \R\text{,}\) the function \(x \mapsto mx\) is continuous.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Fix \(x_0 \in \R\text{.}\) Given \(\ve \gt 0\text{,}\) choose \(\delta = \ve/(1+|m|)\text{.}\) Then \(\delta \gt 0\text{,}\) and whenever \(|x-x_0| \lt \delta\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} |mx-mx_0| = |m||x-x_0| \lt (1+|m|)\delta = \ve. \end{equation*}
