Proposition A.1.5.

Every infinite subset of \(\N\) is countably infinite.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Let \(A\) be an infinite subset of \(\N\text{.}\) The inclusion \(A\hookrightarrow\N\) is injective, so \(|A|\le|\N|\text{.}\) Since \(A\) is infinite, Proposition A.1.3 gives \(|\N|\le|A|\text{.}\) Therefore \(|\N|=|A|\) by Theorem A.1.4.
